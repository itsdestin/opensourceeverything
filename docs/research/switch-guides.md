# Switch guides: method and coverage

Data: `switch-guides.json` (checked 2026-10-27). Apps come from each group's `closed` list in `groups.json` (295 unique; keys use the site's `appSlug`).

**Method.** For each app I fetched the vendor's official help page, then the alternative's own import docs, and wrote only what those pages said. Where a page could not be fetched (403/404/certificate error) or did not say, the field is left empty rather than guessed. `tag` links to a feature-tags-draft id where one matches.

**Coverage.** 138 entries: 69 consumer guides, each with export steps (33 also list at least one verified alternative under `opensIn`), plus 69 `skip: "business service"` (cloud databases, DevOps, analytics, CRM/ERP, messaging APIs).
Done earlier: Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Photos, Calendar, Gmail, Notion, 1Password, LastPass, Slack, Trello, Facebook, Instagram (thin), Spotify (thin), Chrome (bookmarks only).
Added in the second run (from official help pages or search summaries of them): Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive; Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, InDesign, Acrobat; Canva, WhatsApp, Discord, Dropbox, iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, Todoist, Zoom, Google Maps, Keep, Meet, Forms; Reddit, YouTube (thin), Netflix, Goodreads, Grammarly, Mailchimp, Typeform, Calendly, Doodle, Medium, Substack, ChatGPT, GitHub; Dashlane, Authy, Telegram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, Airtable, Figma, Asana, Jira, Shopify, Snapchat, Facebook Messenger. Twitter/X and Evernote are now complete.
Not done: about 157 apps (mostly consumer), including Apple Pages/Numbers/Keynote, Microsoft 365/Office and Google Workspace (suites), Midjourney (no bulk-download page found), Obsidian, Roam, other video editors (Final Cut, DaVinci, CapCut), 3D/CAD, audio, games stores, streaming services other than Netflix, VPNs and antivirus, AI coding tools, and the translation-management and POS apps. Fields left empty (formats, opensIn) mean I could not verify them.

**Caveats for editors.** Google Drive/Gmail "formats" are not stated on Google's page. LastPass export steps come from Bitwarden's import guide, not LastPass's page. Skipped business apps were classified by name, not each checked.
Second-run caveats: Adobe pages returned 403 to direct fetch, so Adobe steps come from search-result summaries of helpx.adobe.com pages. The Authy and Asana/Jira guides rely on the alternative's docs (Ente, Plane) because the vendor offers no export page I could read. Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, Google Maps and Google Keep pages do not state file formats. Apps whose alternatives showed no verified import docs (WhatsApp, Discord, Reddit, Photoshop-to-GIMP, Premiere, Acrobat, Dropbox) have an empty `opensIn`.
