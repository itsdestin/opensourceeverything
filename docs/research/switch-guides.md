# Switch guides: method and coverage

Data: `switch-guides.json` (checked 2026-10-27). Apps come from each group's `closed` list in `groups.json` (295 unique; keys use the site's `appSlug`).

**Method.** For each app I fetched the vendor's official help page, then the alternative's own import docs, and wrote only what those pages said. Where a page could not be fetched (403/404/certificate error) or did not say, the field is left empty rather than guessed. `tag` links to a feature-tags-draft id where one matches.

**Coverage.** 216 entries: 110 consumer guides with export steps (53 also list at least one verified alternative under `opensIn`), 103 `skip: "business service"` (cloud databases, DevOps, analytics, CRM/ERP, messaging APIs, plus work-management, e-signature, support, automation, CMS, translation-management, POS, accounting and remote-access tools; the `tip` field holds the one-line reason), and 3 `skip: "suite"` (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Office: see each app's own guide).
Done earlier: Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Photos, Calendar, Gmail, Notion, 1Password, LastPass, Slack, Trello, Facebook, Instagram (thin), Spotify (thin), Chrome (bookmarks only). Second run: Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive; Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, InDesign, Acrobat; Canva, WhatsApp, Discord, Dropbox, iCloud Photos/Drive, Todoist, Zoom, Google Maps, Keep, Meet, Forms; Reddit, YouTube, Netflix, Goodreads, Grammarly, Mailchimp, Typeform, Calendly, Doodle, Medium, Substack, ChatGPT, GitHub; Dashlane, Authy, Telegram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, Airtable, Figma, Asana, Jira, Shopify, Snapchat, Messenger; Twitter/X, Evernote.
Third run (this one; sources are vendor help pages or search summaries of them): Apple Pages, Numbers, Keynote, Obsidian, Roam Research, Outlook Calendar, Safari, Edge, SurveyMonkey, Threads, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve (community forum only), CapCut (thin), GarageBand, Procreate, Affinity Photo (thin), Google Authenticator, Claude, Gemini, Feedly, Inoreader, Pocket (service closed), YNAB, Monarch Money, Quicken, Toggl, Clockify, Apple Music, Twitch, Loom, Steam (thin), Google Translate, Mendeley, EndNote, Meetup, Visual Studio Code, SketchUp, AutoCAD, Splitwise, Backblaze, DeepL. `opensIn` was also filled for Word, Excel, Evernote, Notion, Slack, Photoshop, Google Photos, Google Drive and OneDrive. Spotify still has none (no verified import docs).
Not done: 79 apps, listed here: TeamSpeak, iMessage, Grindr, Badoo, Lovoo, Clip Studio Paint, Affinity Designer/Publisher, CorelDRAW, Sketch, Adobe XD/Express/After Effects/Audition, Vegas Pro, Maya, Cinema 4D, 3ds Max, FL Studio, Ableton Live, Camtasia, Streamlabs, Noun Project, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, the four OS entries (Windows, macOS, Android, iOS), Google Play and Apple App Store, Gboard, SwiftKey, Google Search, Bing, Keybase, the VPN and antivirus apps, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Codex app, Claude Cowork, ChatGPT agent, Midjourney, DALL-E, Otter.ai, Rev, Perplexity, Plex, Emby, Apple Podcasts, Kindle, Audible, YouTube Live, Google News, the three phone galleries, Epic, GOG, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video, WeTransfer, Time Machine, Apple Maps, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, Empower, Speedtest.net. For Apple Maps, Google Home/Alexa/SmartThings and Kindle, searches found no official export page. Fields left empty (formats, opensIn) mean I could not verify them.

**Caveats for editors.** Google Drive/Gmail "formats" are not stated on Google's page. LastPass export steps come from Bitwarden's import guide, not LastPass's page. Skipped business apps were classified by name, not each checked.
Second-run caveats: Adobe pages returned 403 to direct fetch, so Adobe steps come from search-result summaries of helpx.adobe.com pages. The Authy and Asana/Jira guides rely on the alternative's docs (Ente, Plane) because the vendor offers no export page I could read. Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, Google Maps and Google Keep pages do not state file formats. Apps whose alternatives showed no verified import docs (WhatsApp, Discord, Reddit, Photoshop-to-GIMP, Premiere, Acrobat, Dropbox) have an empty `opensIn`.
Third-run caveats: skipped suites and business apps were classified by name. Roam steps come from Obsidian's import page (Roam's page could not be read), and its Markdown/EDN formats come from forum posts. DaVinci Resolve steps come from Blackmagic forum threads. Firefly III (budgeting), Spliit (Splitwise import is not released) and other alternatives without a docs page that names the vendor's export are left out of `opensIn`. Several vendor pages were old or from search summaries; menu names may differ.
