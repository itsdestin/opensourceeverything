Three ways to show downloads and screenshots

Same six real projects each time. Pick A, B or C for the cards on group pages. The bottom card shows the project page with its screenshot gallery, which comes with any of them.

AScreenshot on every card, one download button in the corner (like the Marketplace). Website and source code are on the project page.

darktable

GPL-3.0★ 13.3k on GitHub

darktable is a free program for organizing and editing raw photos on your own computer. It is aimed at photographers who want a non-destructive workflow for developing and exporting their images.

Penpot

MPL-2.0★ 61.7k on GitHub

Penpot is an open-source design tool for drawing screens, websites and app interfaces, alone or with a team in real time. It can be used in the browser or self-hosted on your own servers, and is aimed at design teams and developers.

Joplin

License being checked★ 56.9k on GitHub

Joplin is a free note-taking and to-do app that keeps your notes on your own devices, so they work offline. It is for people who want to write, organise and search notes in Markdown and sync them across devices with end-to-end encryption.

Shotcut

GPL-3.0★ 15.5k on GitHub

Shotcut is a free video editor you install on your computer to cut, arrange and edit video clips. It is aimed at people who want a full-featured editor without paying for one.

Logseq

AGPL-3.0★ 45.4k on GitHub

Logseq is a note-taking and knowledge management app where you write linked notes, tasks and PDF annotations. It is aimed at students, professionals and anyone who wants to organise ideas and notes.

Organic Maps

Apache-2.0★ 15.8k on GitHub

Organic Maps is a free maps and navigation app for phones that works offline and does not track its users. It is for hikers, cyclists, drivers and travellers who want turn-by-turn directions and maps based on OpenStreetMap.
